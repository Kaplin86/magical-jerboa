# Magical Jerboa
>(Also known as "That Time I Got REINCARNATED As A MAGICAL JERBOA")

Magical jerboa is a roguelite-eqsue platformer where you play as a jerboa who can cast spells. Every two rooms you can unlock a new spell to help you traverse.

## Controls
- Movement - W A S D
- Spell Blue - Q
- Spell Red - E

## Playing
You can play on [itch.io](https://kaplin86.itch.io/magical-jerboa)
